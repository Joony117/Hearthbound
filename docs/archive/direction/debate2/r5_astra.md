**A — Desire paths become the town’s counter-orders**

**Trigger:** repeated live movement wears a route; residents with a shared purpose begin maintaining it. **Rule:** footfall establishes the path, voluntary labor improves it, and disuse lets it fade. A coalition can build through its own contributions and occupied spaces. Blocking a route creates a grievance only among people who discover the obstruction and attribute responsibility.

**Escalation:** when the town walks, maintained routes determine how living crews reach its stations. Residents can physically reroute the city’s response: your siege command becomes an evacuation because the people operating its legs choose the homes they promised to protect.

**Screen:** muddy footprints become paving, murals and gathering places; the piloted lens reveals their meaning to someone involved.

**Decide:** support, negotiate with or confront the counter-plan. **Feel:** discovering a society inside your construction. **Loop:** follow the next path residents make. **Links:** placement, knowledge, defiance, walking town.

**B — Nemesis promotion reveals a particular person**

Give every newly spawned enemy a persistent individual identity **before** they matter. Existing `enemy:<archetype>` records remain unidentified; they cannot establish that two encounters involved the same person.

**Exact promotion rule:** reveal a personal profile after that individual’s **first qualifying event**:

- They cause a final hero death through a recorded causal chain.
- They rescue a warband member from a downing or capture.
- They survive a marked personal confrontation through an actual escape.
- They participate in a surrender, captivity, release or negotiated mercy.

A dead enemy can become historically famous, but only a surviving or escaped individual can return. Promotion is recognition, never resurrection.

**They carry:** their established name; event-earned epithet; actual wound scars; witnessed openings and fallible predictions; grudges; known promises; warband relationships; and legends held by identifiable believers. Their biography uses their own events.

**Between encounters:** recovery, training, recruitment and succession are live-world actions. A scar may motivate learning a counter; it does not grant unexplained knowledge. Their next appearance replaces a generic wave group with a warband pursuing a reachable objective from its actual home. Scouts, insignia and intercepted accounts announce that movement.

**Retirement:** a surviving nemesis who fulfills or revises their ambition can found a school. Pupils inherit taught techniques and testimony; the founder remains present, fallible and socially active.

**The Enemy’s Chronicle:** a diary records its author’s knowledge, omissions and loyalties. “Their healer commands them” becomes a targeting doctrine only after readers accept it. Release a prisoner with a convincing false account, then track its actual transmission; they may embellish, challenge or betray your intended story.

**Do:** exploit their model of you—or change it through mercy and encounters. **Screen:** a recovered sketch becomes recognizable in their next formation. **Feel:** rivalry with someone learning alongside you. **Loop:** discover what they concluded from the last fight. **Links:** knowledge, combat, traditions, belief.

**C — The Guest arranges opportunities**

A candidate encounter requires existing participants, a reachable place, a causal opportunity and unresolved stakes. **Dream collision** means two ambitions require incompatible uses of the same person, place, resource or time window. Misunderstood incompatibility also qualifies.

The Guest scores candidates by personality, then schedules concrete actions: an invitation, messenger, opened route, exposed object or sponsored testimony. Those actions enter the Ledger. Outcomes emerge through participants’ choices. Repetition reduces a candidate’s novelty; previous resolutions change what the Guest tries next.

- **Mother Briar — attachment under contradiction.** She prioritizes strong relationships whose dreams obstruct each other. **Omen:** two familiar ribbons knot around one destination. She brings both claimants there with credible opportunities to succeed. **Do:** choose a side or construct a third future both must genuinely accept. **Feel:** affection demanding invention. **Loop:** discover what their changed relationship now makes possible. **Links:** Bonds, Dreams, Founding. Briar can arrange a magnificent joint celebration when the collision produces something neither imagined.

- **The Gilded Jackal — public commitments against difficult outcomes.** He announces a precise proposition and stakes a real boon against it. His odds express wager terms, not a claim of perfect prediction; longer odds carry greater declared stakes. **Omen:** named figures enact the wager on his table. He can sponsor opposition, but cannot alter the proposition after commitment. The Ledger settles the result; his pledged boon must become available. **Do:** exploit his assumptions and actual interventions. **Feel:** competitive audacity. **Loop:** win the advantage needed to challenge his next wager. **Links:** combat, belief, promises.

- **Sister Cinder — unfinished history seeking a living consequence.** She prioritizes neglected obligations and memories about to lose their last accessible witness. **Omen:** ash traces an old route toward a present household. She brings surviving evidence and affected people together, including accounts that conflict. **Do:** preserve, dispute, fulfill or consciously renounce the obligation before its opportunity passes. **Feel:** responsibility complicated by discovery. **Loop:** find whom the resulting decision reaches. **Links:** afterlife, institutions, Old World.

**D — Stealing the chair means taking an office**

The chair is a unique physical seat at the Guest’s gathering place. Its arms display active omens; its authority lets its occupant arrange future opportunities in that world. The surrounding table holds pledged objects and evidence from resolved schemes.

**Trigger:** a **living Risen** adopts the ambition to take it, resolves a Guest’s declared scheme through their own counter-plan, and gains the support of people materially affected by that outcome.

**Rule:** those supporters can demand a public reckoning. The Guest must leave the seat to answer its outstanding commitments. The Risen must reach and occupy it while supporters bring their testimony and ratify a new covenant. The Guest contests the attempt using actual allies, debts and available interventions. Killing a boss alone cannot transfer the office.

**Screen:** a battle moves through the tavern and town while ordinary residents carry the objects your campaign made significant. Each delivered testimony changes an omen carved into the chair.

**Decide:** help the Risen, bargain over their proposed covenant or oppose their claim. **Feel:** pride and alarm at someone outgrowing your command. **Loop:** gather the people whose support the attempt still needs. **Links:** Dreams, knowledge, defiance, Founding.

After victory, that same living hero leaves the combat roster to occupy the office. Their values, grudges and understanding of you determine their scheduling priorities. At succession, they carry the office into the next town as its Guest. The displaced Guest remains a person with memories and grievances, deprived of that authority.

**E — Stolen names sever recognition**

**Trigger:** a nemesis acquires a personal anchor and successfully performs a name-stealing rite.

**Rule:** the curse disconnects memories of deeds from their bearer in affected people. Sensory details, habits and learned techniques survive; the identity and original events remain intact in the Ledger. Retelling the cursed account can spread that failure of recognition.

The thief builds a signature sequence from the stolen reputations. Each captive name sustains a distinct part of it.

**Screen:** the victim’s face loses its familiar recognition cues through an affected hero’s lens. Their friend still sets out two bowls. During the nemesis’s heavy, a movement looks painfully familiar.

**Decide:** assemble the surviving connections: a witness, a storied object and someone able to perform the remembered technique. During the confrontation, that technique can answer the matching stolen sequence and expose its seal. Break the seal, and witnesses recover the association; they must carry the restored account home.

For a dead victim, a living apprentice performs the technique. Recovery restores a name and its relationships, never a playable life.

**Feel:** recognizing someone before the world can explain why. **Loop:** free another name and reunite its scattered witnesses. **Links:** missing beats, memory items, grief, Chronicle, combat.
