**A — The Last Watch becomes a graduation—or an occupation**

The soul’s announced departure starts its final season. **Rule:** a living custodian must learn the relevant technique and demonstrate it at the actual doorway. The protection afterward comes from their own abilities, equipment and practiced formation.

**Screen:** the pupil takes the familiar defensive position while the departing guardian watches. The final lesson becomes a farewell celebration.

**Decide:** organize training, redesign the defense or face the coming absence. **Feel:** pride inseparable from goodbye. **Loop:** complete the pupil’s next demonstration. **Links:** mentorship, institutions, combat, afterlife.

A coercive rite acts **before Released**, forcibly maintaining captivity. Escalate its consequence: the imprisoned guardian interprets its protective purpose through what it witnesses. It starts sheltering residents from your enforcers, closing routes against your champion. When it becomes a hollow, your own defensive geometry becomes its encounter.

**Do/feel:** dismantle the prison you built; face responsibility made physical. **Loop:** restore the possibility of farewell. **Links:** defiance, knowledge, hollows. Final release remains irreversible.

**B — Five joy mechanics**

1. **The Unkillable Chicken.**  
   **Trigger:** retellings associate a hero’s embarrassing escapes with a nickname. **Rule:** distinct believers sustain it; the hero’s stance determines whether they embrace its manifested skill. The resulting defensive ability grants a brief, single-hit protection window that prevents a downing before it occurs. It uses the ordinary counter picker, cooldown and ability lock. **Screen:** the solemn knight performs the ridiculous escape everyone imitates. **Decide:** cultivate, reinterpret or challenge the nickname. **Feel:** affection becoming disbelief when it works. **Loop:** discover the next telling. **Links:** belief, gossip, combat.

2. **Pets Remember What People Explain Away.**  
   **Trigger:** mercy and repeated contact establish attachment to a creature. **Rule:** it retains witnessed sensory associations: scent, motion, places, familiar distress. Those associations provoke behavior; they do not establish guilt. It may growl because the suspected spy once hurt it—or once smelled like its captor. **Screen:** it blocks a doorway, fetches an overlooked object or drags someone toward safety. **Decide:** follow, reenact or investigate the association. **Feel:** tenderness mixed with curiosity. **Loop:** learn another habit’s meaning. **Links:** evidence, rescue, spies. Reading its warning can change an expedition’s route.

3. **The Door’s Terrible Impersonator.**  
   **Trigger:** a resident imitates a memorable order during recreation. **Rule:** listeners repeat the impression according to their attitudes; performances explicitly record actor and portrayed person. Some audiences recognize satire, while later tellers may mistake it for testimony. **Screen:** a tiny wooden Door orders everyone to protect a stolen soup pot. **Decide:** attend, answer the criticism or lend props and let the joke grow. **Feel:** being known well enough to be teased. **Loop:** see the next performance. **Links:** diaries, reputation, gossip. Its popularity changes the chorus influencing who answers summons.

4. **The Masquerade Becomes a Doctrine.**  
   **Trigger:** residents swap costumes and roles for a festival performance. **Rule:** observers learn disguise cues through what they witness; clothing transfers neither skills nor identity. Practiced performers can carry that deception into battle against opponents whose recognition depends on those cues. **Screen:** the knight attracts attention in the healer’s sash while the real healer plays their attendant. **Decide:** rehearse the exchange and exploit an enemy’s mistaken targeting before actual casts expose it. **Feel:** shared mischief. **Loop:** devise another role swap. **Links:** knowledge, enemy chronicles, combat.

5. **The Wrong Person’s Invention.**  
   **Trigger:** a workshop experiment produces an awkward item variant: ceremonial boots that launch the wearer whenever they bow. **Rule:** its actual effect persists; another resident’s habit or technique can give it a useful application. Practicing that application develops an equipment skill using ordinary combat timing. **Screen:** a humiliated dignitary watches the rogue bow directly into a perfect flank. **Decide:** give the invention to someone who sees its possibility. **Feel:** delighted discovery. **Loop:** find a person for the next peculiar creation. **Links:** passions, crafting, skills, living achievements.

**C — Bonds are overlapping relationships with evidence**

Each directed relationship retains **familiarity, affection, situational trust, respect, grievances, commitments and remembered shared techniques**. Mira can trust Oren’s shield, resent his politics and owe him a rescue simultaneously.

Encounters change these through the participants’ interpretations. One consequential rescue can transform trust immediately; repeated identical contact does not automatically deepen it. Relationships can be asymmetric.

- **Friend:** seeks company and shares news; prioritizes opportunities to protect or rescue.
- **Rival:** pursues a competing goal and studies performance; can respect a counter, coordinate brilliantly or refuse a particular arrangement.
- **Partner/bondmate:** forms reciprocal commitments and shared plans; those promises shape courage reservations and abandonment refusals.
- **Mentor/pupil:** agrees to teach and practice particular techniques; creates inherited openings and the missing beat after departure or death.
- **Grudge:** attributes harm to someone; influences avoidance, confrontation and willingness to assist.
- **Debt:** records a specific perceived obligation; repayment, forgiveness or disputed interpretation changes what the hero will undertake.

**Concordance belongs to a pair and a practiced technique.** Successful coordination across varied situations establishes it. Friendship can encourage practice; rivals can achieve it too. Its sacrifice multiplier consumes a relationship’s developed potential, regardless of affection.

**Do:** arrange opportunities and respond to actual commitments. **Screen:** voluntary visits, avoided seats and combat connections express the relationship. **Feel:** learning people rather than filling an affection bar. **Loop:** see what the next shared experience changes. **Links:** town, knowledge, combat, sacrifice.

**D — Diaries explain the person you return to**

While you watch a battle, residents continue live routines. Moods arise from specific experiences; private goals select available actions; repeated actions become habits. A secret is information they have not shared, coupled with reasons to conceal it.

**Trigger:** an event matters enough to a diary-keeping hero to reflect on it. **Rule:** an entry combines what they know, what they infer and what they intend. It never supplies facts they could not possess.

> “You kept me home again. I think you’re ashamed of me. Tomorrow I’ll ask her to teach me.”

The player knows they were protecting that hero. The next entry depends on what happens, not which reassuring response earns points.

**Screen:** first show the consequence—training gear outside another house, an extra chair, an unfinished letter. The diary provides their interpretation.

**Decide:** bring evidence, change an order, visit or let their plan unfold. **Feel:** affection, surprise or being misunderstood. **Loop:** after several minutes elsewhere, return to discover one changed intention and its physical consequence. **Links:** Dreams, teaching, refusal, defection, Door reputation.

**E — Sports and potlucks become practiced relationships**

**Squad sport: Bell Run.** Teams carry a bell between moving objectives and ring it at the opposing rooftop. Training impacts tag contestants out while preserving ordinary targeting, counter opportunities, cooldowns and the ability lock.

Paired techniques progress toward Concordance when one contestant creates an opening and the other successfully uses it. Varied opponents, routes and exchanged roles deepen learning; repeating one arrangement has diminishing value.

**Do:** arrange teams, program preferences and pilot a contestant. **Screen:** the pair’s familiar combat ribbon completes as a ridiculous diving pass wins the match. **Feel:** competitive affection. **Loop:** challenge another school. **Links:** skills, rivalry, Concordance, festivals.

**Rift potluck.** A host invites known homeland contacts; each brings a recipe, preferences and stories. Ingredients must be obtained and dishes actually prepared. Seating determines encounters; taste and remembered hospitality influence what participants discuss.

Accepted teaching passes recipes to residents. Learned dishes become food and expedition provisions made from their real ingredients; enthusiastic retellings also alter the summoning chorus.

**Do:** source an ingredient, choose hosts and arrange the table. **Screen:** an enemy veteran and your apprentice argue passionately about dumplings. **Feel:** belonging across an unlikely divide. **Loop:** invite the person mentioned over dinner. **Links:** food, economy, Rifts, knowledge.
