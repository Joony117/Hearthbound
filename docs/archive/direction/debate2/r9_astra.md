**2. The Founder Walks Again**

**Trigger:** a living retiree consents to their once-per-life legendary muster. **Rule:** actual pupils travel from their institutions and take formation roles using learned skills. The founder’s signals coordinate preferred responses; every participant retains ordinary cooldowns, locks and counter priorities.

**Feel:** witnessing a lifetime become an army. **Loop:** cultivate the traditions whose members can answer that call. **Links:** Founding, mentorship, skills, retirement.

**3. The rescue chain**

**Trigger:** a known commitment prioritizes a downed person. **Rule:** rescue becomes a persistent objective: create separation, reach the person, complete the existing carry interaction, then extract. Each step is reevaluated, never queued blindly. After lifting, carrying permits compatible spells and movements; skills needing unavailable hands require setting the person down. Casting obeys normal timing and cannot bypass the lift channel.

**Screen:** the carrier casts backward while a partner opens the escape route.

**Decide:** clear pursuit, support the carrier or change extraction routes. **Feel:** coordinated desperation. **Loop:** train the combination that brings someone home next time. **Links:** Bonds, promises, skills, rescue.

**4. Independent settlements and the congress**

**Decide:** negotiate, sponsor an alternative agreement or accept the consequences of refusing. **Feel:** pride mixed with lost ownership. **Loop:** revisit what those people built and promised. **Links:** Founding, Heralds, Old World, living exits.

**E — Rulings check**

Two formulations require explicit repair:

- **Dream projections:** they must be views of the same living actor in a dreaming state, never independently acting copies. Death of that person ends their participation.
- **Imported continuations:** an older or divergent file cannot revive a dead identity or duplicate a present soul. Compatible histories can continue the campaign; conflicting accounts can enter as testimony and fuel disputed legends, while incompatible actual continuations remain separate.

The remaining design preserves the rulings:

- Dead souls never become selectable combatants; Parliament and guardian effects act through their current vessels.
- One soul has one presence; Released is terminal.
- All clocks—including travel, memory, winter and older worlds—advance only during live simulation.
- Old Worlds persist; living departures can change allegiance again.
- Only Risen can claim the chair.
- Living deeds and sacrifice both produce power.
- Refusal and overthrow remain real, consequential possibilities.
