**Enemy belief makes mercy, captivity and victory change the shape of power.** A captive who comes to trust the knight can become the first believer in a protective version of the feared legend. Their conversion changes their response; it does not convert the entire warband. **Do:** arrange encounters, exchange prisoners or cultivate terror. **Feel:** dependence on people you could destroy. **Loop:** discover what survives their changed opinion. **Links:** capture, gossip, legends, defiance.

**Accept the piloted hero as the lens.** Their brother’s face overlays the nemesis; their remembered partner’s empty position appears beside them. Switching pilot reveals incompatible meanings while positions, targeting silhouettes and attack geometry remain consistent. Switching never transfers knowledge. **Do:** recognize the disagreement, then get a witness or signal to the hero who needs it. **Feel:** inhabiting another person’s understanding. **Loop:** learn why another squadmate sees this fight differently. **Links:** knowledge, counters, missing beats.

**A — The Door listens to a chorus**

**Trigger:** a paid summon: **100 Stones**, earned through expeditions.

**Rule:** draw ordinary rank from the unchanged weights. Then conversation influences which eligible person answers: current topics favor associated homelands, relationships, ambitions and adversaries. Each resident contributes a bounded influence weighted by conviction, recall and connection to the subject. Repeating one speaker cannot drown out the whole town.

A song about a dragon might attract its hunter, keeper, escaped servant or someone blamed for awakening it. Conversation determines a field of human connections; it never selects a product.

**Screen:** approaching the Door, you hear overlapping fragments from actual residents. Their claim emblems become silhouettes beyond the rift. The final glimpse shows a person doing something before rank and identity resolve.

**Decide:** sponsor festivals, publish accounts, invite speakers, suppress dissent or pursue the people needed to change the conversation.

**Pity:** after the rank drought reaches its first threshold, one high-rank traveler becomes identifiable. Subsequent pulls advance a visible approach toward a fixed guarantee; changing topics cannot erase it. The guarantee is an explicit exception to ordinary rank weights.

**Feel:** anticipation shaped by a town with competing desires. **Loop:** one expedition funds another arrival; that arrival changes the chorus. **Links:** Dreams, gossip, homelands, pursuers.

**B — Keep x3; make it recognition, not a surname**

A shared definition describes a kind of hero. It cannot establish that two people are siblings. Replace that automatic equivalence with **Concordance**.

**Trigger:** two distinct heroes repeatedly practice and execute a particular shared technique.

**Rule:** once they achieve Concordance, sacrificing either into the other yields the existing **x3 multiplier** on the rank-and-level basis. Kinship can help bring them together; unrelated companions can qualify. Forced sacrifice still produces the burst. Its soul joins the Parliament with everything they remember.

**Screen:** the altar previews the familiar paired animation. On commitment, the donor’s position empties; the recipient performs both halves alone.

**Decide:** preserve a productive partnership or consume it for immediate ascent.

**The town responds through knowledge.** Witnesses tell what happened. Pupils can abandon the recipient’s school, a bondmate can hide the intended donor, and former students can found a rival tradition specifically trained to break the champion’s stolen combination. Others may defend a purposeful volunteer’s choice.

**Feel:** recognizing exactly what the shortcut destroys. **Loop:** develop partnerships whose living achievements and sacrificial potential become increasingly consequential. **Links:** skills, living Essence, Parliament, protest, rival towns.

**C — Story creates obligations as well as power**

**Keep both proposed guardrails.** Living Essence never generates Stones: expeditions keep drawing the town into other people’s worlds. Sacrifice remains the faster conversion into SSS from comparable starting resources. Its aftermath changes the campaign; it does not guarantee the fastest resolution of every subsequent problem.

**Reject “unwitnessed deeds earn nothing.”** It contradicts the overlooked F-rank and makes private devotion worthless. Real, consequential deeds earn living Essence whether applauded or secret.

Replace it with something wilder: **a lie can finance a miracle that its believers later demand you perform.**

**Trigger:** a spreading claim inspires a concrete communal undertaking—a school, pilgrimage, monument or rescue pledge.

**Rule:** that undertaking can produce real Essence once. Its provenance names the claim and participants. Repeated retellings cannot mint it again. Spending it creates real advancement; exposure never reverses past deaths or rewrites earned ranks.

When conviction in the claim collapses, betrayed believers can manifest **The Trial of the Boast**. Their promised bridge, dragon or impossible siege becomes a present battlefield generated from the legend. The champion may attempt the feat now, confess and negotiate restitution, or fight the institution demanding repayment. Performing it now does not make the original account true.

The Guest can sponsor false testimony and arrange exposure; it cannot forge the Ledger itself.

**Screen:** the monument opens into the ordeal it commemorated. **Feel:** your shortcut becoming an adventure with named creditors. **Loop:** confront the consequence and decide who the champion becomes. **Links:** belief, Founding, Guest, combat, overthrow.

**The whole loop:** expeditions earn Stones and produce deeds; Stones summon people whose lives alter the town’s conversations; deeds and fulfilled ambitions earn Essence, while sacrifice converts a person’s potential faster. Essence buys rank-ups. Belief shapes special powers and who answers the Door, while collective undertakings can turn stories into further growth. Every shortcut risks relationships, obligations and the institutions that make those powers possible.

**D — The Door has a body, dependencies and a future**

- **Loved.** **Trigger:** people repeatedly witness the Door honor promises that matter to them. **Rule:** residents initiate a covenant and volunteer anchors, guards or diplomatic service according to their ambitions. **Screen:** they construct a public house around your threshold, with a place for every household. **Decide:** accept the reciprocal commitments or reject particular terms. **Feel:** belonging. **Loop:** fulfill the next promise. **Links:** Dreams, Founding, summoning.

- **Hunted.** **Trigger:** survivors or messengers carry evidence of your location and interventions. **Rule:** enemies pursue your physical anchor; capturing it lets them constrain where you open and whom they permit through. **Screen:** their siege apparatus fastens onto your threshold. **Decide:** defend, relocate, misdirect the pursuit or bargain. **Feel:** vulnerable dependence on the roster. **Loop:** learn who is following your trail. **Links:** Nemesis, knowledge, raids.

- **Rescued.** **Trigger:** your anchor is captured and free people retain a commitment to you. **Rule:** those people assemble a liberation expedition from their actual allies and resources. You command or pilot willing rescuers under their own commitments. **Screen:** someone you once saved breaks into the chamber carrying your old household banner. **Decide:** help their chosen approach or negotiate another. **Feel:** reciprocity. **Loop:** reunite the scattered community. **Links:** Bonds, rescue, institutions.

- **Overthrown.** **Trigger:** an informed coalition forms around a grievance, gains backing across institutions and physically seizes the anchor. **Rule:** it revokes your authority and imposes a settlement: a binding covenant, confinement or expulsion into a successor chapter. **Screen:** your own streets become the coalition’s approach routes. **Decide:** resist, concede, negotiate or support its independence. **Feel:** consequence—or pride mixed with loss. **Loop:** meet the society they build afterward. **Links:** defiance, Parliament, Old World.

After expulsion, the same Door reaches the next world; the old town persists as an independent homeland. Its people carry their accounts of you. You can **want** an overthrow because you raised founders capable of governing themselves—and still discover that their chosen future rejects your preferred successor. Liberation must belong to them.
