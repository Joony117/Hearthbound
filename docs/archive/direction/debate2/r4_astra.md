**A — The Door carries accountability, not ownership of the dead**

Keep the permanent debt, but distinguish **where a soul resides** from **whom it can hold responsible**. Otherwise moving the Parliament into the Door strips the old champion of the Parliament they were supposed to bring back as a nemesis.

**Trigger:** a sacrifice or covenant binds identifiable people to the Door’s actions. **Rule:** the soul retains one presence—in a champion, hollow or other established afterlife state—but can send testimony through the Door into dreams. Each transmission becomes testimony with a source, recipients and the soul’s fallible understanding. A claim ends through fulfillment or the claimant’s explicit release.

**Screen:** when a new anchor awakens, its threshold fills with unfinished promises spoken in familiar voices. Existing homeland residents and approaching recruits receive those dreams; an empty settlement cannot already have gossip.

**Decide:** fulfill, contest, conceal or negotiate the debt. **Feel:** recognition before reinvention. **Loop:** approach another world knowing someone there may already have heard your dead. **Links:** Parliament, knowledge, Old World, covenants.

**Keep the plants—and let them develop attachments.** Captors route an actual agent through a summon. That person reports only what they learn; sending information requires a messenger, meeting or rift connection. Witnesses can expose their conduct, while friendship can make them withhold intelligence. **Do:** investigate encounters, confront evidence or turn the spy against their handler. **Feel:** suspicion becoming possible trust. **Loop:** discover which loyalty wins their next decision. **Links:** gossip, captivity, Bonds. The player may know before residents do; exposure still requires reaching them.

**B — Placement creates encounters; encounters create relationships**

**Trigger:** placing a house or workplace changes reachable neighbors and commuting routes.

**Rule on the hex grid:**

- Occupied homes sharing an edge gain recurring doorstep encounter opportunities during live town routines.
- Speech across a shared wall can reach nearby residents; it transmits audible claims, never private thoughts.
- A home-to-work route creates encounters at the doors and gathering places it passes.
- Each resident has limited attention per outing. Interests and relationships decide whom they approach; adjacency alone never grants friendship.
- Existing workplace slots, one-home ownership and **passion XP ×4** remain intact.

**Before placement:** named residents walk translucent versions of their proposed routes. Doorstep circles show likely meeting places; audible conversation emblems demonstrate where gossip could travel. A crush’s petition and a rival’s avoidance visibly pull toward different sites.

**Decide:** provide proximity, privacy, opportunity or separation. Accepting a move petition initiates packing and a physical moving day; the single home assignment changes on arrival. Furniture, helpers and bystanders make that move a remembered event.

**Feel:** anticipation about who will meet—and responsibility when you separate them. **Loop:** visit the street after its next routine. **Links:** Bonds, knowledge, passions, spies, Dreams.

**C — A storied place preserves evidence and teaches a practice**

**Trigger:** a consequential event occurs at a building.

**Rule:** the place retains a material imprint of local details: a shield groove, scorched beam, interrupted workpiece. Rites can recover sensory fragments; witnesses interpret them. Repeated commemorations turn a scar into a custom, and practiced customs can become institutional techniques.

**Screen:** the piloted survivor sees the doorway’s remembered defensive position; another resident sees the version taught at its annual ceremony.

**Decide:** restore the site, reinterpret its commemoration, or dismantle it. Demolition destroys the last accessible physical evidence if nothing was preserved or retold; the immutable Ledger still records what happened.

Escalate salvage: **move the witness itself**. Carry the marked lintel into a refugee quarter, where its history can teach new residents—or expose their celebrated founder.

**Feel:** handling something that mattered to someone. **Loop:** bring another witness or pupil to the place. **Links:** knowledge, institutions, afterlife. These taught district techniques supply the walking town’s actions; destruction cannot be a free history reset.

**D — Dreams are desired changes with personal conditions**

A dream contains **owner, formative events, desired change, named targets or beneficiaries, acceptable methods, observable milestones, and the owner’s reason**. It forms from what the hero knows, infers and cares about.

The hero reveals it through behavior and speech: saved materials, repeated practice, a request at dinner. New evidence can revise its target or methods. Actual achievements earn living Essence; believing an achievement happened remains a separate state. A dream that establishes an ongoing practice can found an institution, which creates opportunities for other people.

Three generated arcs:

- **Woodcutting — “Give us a road home.”**  
  **Trigger:** a woodcutter witnesses a bondmate endangered on a detour. **Do:** supply timber and build a raised connection between their home and workplace. Refugees arriving beyond it can broaden the dream into connecting their homes too. **Proof:** the named residents complete the formerly dangerous journey. **Payoff:** Essence and a bridgewright school; its pupils eventually construct the walking town’s crossing limbs. **Screen:** the bondmate carries the first meal across; later, the route lifts into the air. **Feel:** domestic care becoming grandeur. **Loop:** connect the next household. **Links:** placement, Founding, walking town.

- **Smithing — “This shield brings you back.”**  
  **Trigger:** an apprentice returns with a shield broken by an observed heavy attack. **Do:** recover its fragments and forge a response to that failure. The apprentice wants revenge, so the smith can revise the design into protection that creates an opening for the apprentice’s counter. **Proof:** they execute that sequence against the relevant attack and return together. **Payoff:** Essence and a school teaching the paired technique. **Screen:** the old fracture lights along the successful parry. **Feel:** relief and shared authorship. **Loop:** develop the next pupil’s answer. **Links:** memory items, Concordance, combat.

- **Rites — “Let them remember whom they buried.”**  
  **Trigger:** a rites practitioner hears conflicting accounts of a grave’s occupant. **Do:** gather living witnesses, recover a place’s imprint and hear the departed testimony. Evidence can change a dream of glorification into one of recording an uncomfortable life honestly. **Proof:** establish the account and hold the promised rite; unanimous approval is unnecessary. **Payoff:** Essence and a Hall of Witnesses that convenes future disputes. **Screen:** mourners disagree, then place different objects at the same grave. **Feel:** intimacy without easy absolution. **Loop:** hear whose account arrives next winter. **Links:** memory, afterlife, belief, institutions.

**E — Dread comes from approaching choices**

All progression below uses **live simulation time**. Closing the game freezes hunger, seasons, raids, memory decay and preparations; reopening resumes that state without catch-up.

- **Winter:** falling temperature progressively closes production opportunities and approaches the Winter of Names. **Screen:** fields frost over while old thresholds acquire visitors’ silhouettes. **Decide:** stock food, keep a promised route open or bring its travelers home early. **Feel:** anticipation tangled with dread. **Loop:** finish one more preparation before the live calendar turns. **Links:** farming, promises, afterlife.
- **Hunger:** dwindling reserves collide with household commitments. Residents may share their actual rations, conceal a shortage or contest distribution; food is conserved through every transfer. **Screen:** someone sets two bowls down and quietly leaves theirs untouched. **Decide:** change provisioning, shift workers, forage or negotiate priorities before starvation. **Feel:** concern for a specific person. **Loop:** return to see whether that household ate. **Links:** jobs, Bonds, defiance.
- **Raids:** enemies choose reachable objectives from acquired knowledge and grudges. A spy who knows the evacuation route makes its defense urgent. **Screen:** scouts approach the familiar lane your residents use every morning. **Decide:** recall a squad, relocate vulnerable residents, defend the route or exploit the enemy’s outdated information. **Feel:** recognition of your own layout under threat. **Loop:** repair and redesign around whom it protected. **Links:** placement, spies, nemeses, combat.
