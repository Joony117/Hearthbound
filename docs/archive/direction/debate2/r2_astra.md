**A — Belief becomes a battlefield force**

**Trigger:** enough living people sincerely accept a claim about a particular person.

**Rule:** manifestation depends on distinct believers, conviction and remembered salience. Repeated performances reinforce memory; copying testimony does not create additional believers. Thresholds produce **whisper → legend → miracle**: first a recognizable motif, then a usable skill, then a transformed battlefield effect. Further belief strengthens the existing expression with diminishing gains. Contradictory claims can manifest competing powers.

For **Held the Bridge**, the legend skill raises a spectral crossing whose defender intercepts attacks against those passing behind them. Greater manifestation extends what it can protect. It still has a cooldown and obeys the ability lock.

**Screen:** residents reenact the bridge story with their bodies and lanterns. The same bridge silhouette appears behind the knight when the skill becomes available. Confession changes those performances: some lanterns extinguish; others form a new emblem.

**Decide:** cultivate the story, disclose its origins, or demonstrate a different identity through deeds. The hero’s stance can refuse a miracle that requires them to perform a role they reject.

**Guest exploitation:** seat the original witnesses beside devoted admirers at a celebration. **Nemesis exploitation:** use the miracle’s actual geometry against its bearer—attack both ends of the crossing, forcing a visible choice about whom to protect. Enemies can also cultivate legends through frightened witnesses.

**Feel:** power and identity pulling against each other. **Loop:** return to discover which version of this person the town now believes. **Links:** gossip, Dreams, Guest, Chronicle, combat.

**B — Six person-driven combat rules**

**Common contract:** personal knowledge and commitments determine eligible actions; the picker retains **stun → interrupt → shield → dodge → walk out** among available telegraph answers. Chains prefer eligible skills; they never promise future execution. Every executed skill retains its cooldown and 1.0-second lock. Piloting passes through the same eligibility and refusal rules.

   **Trigger:** a frightened hero considers entering a recognized threat. **Rule:** courage becomes available when a trusted partner has an applicable protective response, valid range and a free cooldown/lock. Their agreement reserves that response; a higher-priority emergency can break the reservation, visibly withdrawing assurance. **Screen:** a braided connection joins the attack and protective skill. **Decide:** position both heroes, then commit or find another opening. **Feel:** deliberate trust. **Loop:** master another partnership. **Links:** Bonds, fear, counters.

   **Trigger:** a manifested skill’s role condition occurs—hold a crossing, protect a surrender, publicly confess a deception. **Rule:** the skill enters the same picker as an eligible shield, counter or other declared effect. **D — One visual grammar: cause → ribbon → action**

Every important personal event uses a short ribbon connecting its source to its consequence. **Shape carries meaning; color reinforces it.**

- **Bond:** braided ribbon.
- **Belief:** a claim emblem travels with the ribbon between speakers.
- **Refusal:** the proposed ribbon breaks; a new one points toward the chosen action.
- **Legend skill:** that same claim emblem enlarges into the ability’s terrain or telegraph.

At battle zoom, full ribbons appear for the selected squad and imminent actions affecting it. Other units show a single consequential seal; inspection unfolds its sources.
