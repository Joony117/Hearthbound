3. Graveyard in town: gravestones with name/rank/kills/cause; friends visit graves.
4. Bonds: heroes who fight together become friends -> cross-hero combo skills; grief when a friend dies.
5. Town raids: monsters attack the town; home heroes defend. Joins town and combat.
6. Apprentices: a long-housed hero trains an apprentice who inherits a passion + a skill

7. The Chronicle: the game auto-writes a history book of your run.
8. Rifts instead of banners: summoning opens portals to other worlds; dangerous rifts give better

9. Heroes with opinions: piloting a terrified hero hesitates; brave one gets a boost.
10. Seasons: winter = no farming; stock up in fall.
