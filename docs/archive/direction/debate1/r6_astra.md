**A — Six pillars, one game**

1. **The Door Opens Onto Lives**  
   **Fantasy:** Every summon brings a person whose arrival changes two worlds.  
   **Contains:** the player as conscious Door; Summon Stones and personal anchors; homeland rifts; dangerous recruits and pursuers; the thirty-second arrival; guaranteed beacon pity; distinct entourage pulls; *The Life They Left Behind*.  
   **Links:** arrivals seed **Home** with passions and dreams, **Battle** with skills, and **Fate** with existing relationships. **Legacy** makes former towns summon destinations.

2. **A Home Worth Returning To**  
   **Fantasy:** Build a town whose streets explain why these people care about each other.  
   **Contains:** houses and neighbors; passions, jobs and collaborative schools; food, shared tables and starvation; seasons; evacuation promises; Bonds and rivalries; Dreams that collide or become shared; Founding, living institutions, dream-seeding retirees and apprentices.  
   **Links:** daily encounters create **Battle** combinations; institutions earn **Power** through living achievement; empty homes give **Legacy** physical meaning. Raids bring all three onto the streets you built.

3. **People Inside the Fight**  
   **Fantasy:** Command a squad whose history changes every opening, counter and rescue.  
   **Contains:** real-time expeditions and raids; autonomous skill selection; large learned kits, programmed chains and single-hero piloting; trusted-partner counters; dream-driven rescues; fallible, legible refusals; fear and opinions; grief’s missing combo beat; nemeses learning your tactics; a retiree’s legendary return and living aftermath.  
   **Links:** **Home** supplies relationships, **Power** supplies earned and inherited abilities, **Fate** supplies personal opponents, and **Legacy** preserves the consequences.

4. **Power Has Witnesses**  
   **Fantasy:** Become extraordinary among people who remember how you did it.  
   **Contains:** F→SSS growth through Essence and deeds; overlooked F-ranks becoming Risen; sacrifice and purposeful volunteers; protest, concealment, substitution and desertion; the Parliament’s conditional cooperation and spectral combat chains; rival settlements; judging, loving, hunting or overthrowing the Door.  
   **Links:** **Home** generates both cooperation and resistance; **Battle** tests promises; **Fate** exploits disputes; **Legacy** carries your champion and their contributors into history.

5. **Fate Has a Face**  
   **Fantasy:** Outwit a supernatural Guest who builds spectacular situations from lives you actually shaped.  
   **Contains:** Mother Briar, the Gilded Jackal and Sister Cinder; omens, bargains and causal revelations; generated dream collisions; named, promoted and scarred nemeses; stolen-name mysteries; grudges and revenge; the Risen hero’s challenge for the chair.  
   **Links:** reads the other five pillars and changes opportunities within them. It cannot invent past relationships, predetermine battle outcomes or manufacture deaths to complete a scene.

6. **Nothing Ends Without Leaving Something**  
   **Fantasy:** Walk through a world altered by everyone who lived here.  
   **Contains:** one continuous afterlife; graves and visits; hollow bosses with real kits and gear; Winter of Names and unfinished promises; the recognizing doorway growing into a living town; Chronicle chapters and conflicting witnesses; inherited traditions. **The Old World**, pending owner approval, extends this into successor towns, former champions as nemeses and a former hero as Guest.  
   **Links:** remembered people redirect **Home**, **Battle** and **Power**; former worlds feed **The Door**; inherited consequences give **Fate** deeper material.
