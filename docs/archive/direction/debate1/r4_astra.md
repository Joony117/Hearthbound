**The Old World earns the title—but it changes the earlier persistent-roster premise.** Make that an explicit creative decision. A fallen town leaves survivors and unfinished business; founding its successor lets you approach that history from the other side of a rift.

**A — Prestige as an expedition into your own history**

- **Carry consequences across, including inconvenient ones.** Names, relationships, institutions, learned techniques, graves, promises and the Parliament persist as world history. Lose direct ownership of that roster, its stockpiles and its territory. Your old champion remains a particular person, with reasons to help or hunt you.
- **The next town begins with an invitation.** Before departure, reveal three successor possibilities generated by the old world: an apprentice founding a forbidden school; refugees fleeing your champion; the nemesis’s child asking for protection. **Do/feel:** choose whose future to inhabit, with anticipation already attached to a person. **Loop:** start another town to answer an invitation you helped create.
- **Your retirement plan becomes geography.** The tavern you founded becomes a sanctuary encounter; the abandoned district becomes hostile territory; your old defensive layout becomes the dungeon you must infiltrate. **Do/feel:** navigate your own choices and recognize what endured. **Loop:** one more expedition to revisit a familiar place changed by its inhabitants.
- **Escalation: generations can confront each other.** Your current squad can rescue an ancestor from your former SSS champion while the chair-thief narrates the encounter with remembered resentment. **Do/feel:** defeat a strategy you once perfected—or convince its surviving participants to break formation. **Loop:** summon someone who remembers another way through. **Feeds:** Rifts, traditions, Nemesis, Parliament, Storyteller.
- **Identity rule:** crossing worlds relocates the same living person. An ancestor’s echo is explicitly an echo. No multiplying one soul into summon fodder while claiming their death mattered.

**B — Five fights only these people could have**

- **1. “I Know Your Next Move.”** Bonded heroes develop a shared chain: one commits to a vulnerable attack; the other’s counter catches the retaliation and turns it into a launch. **Do:** program the pair’s sequence, then pilot either partner through its opening. **Screenshot:** a terrified healer steps into a lethal telegraph because the knight’s shield is already coming. **Feel:** trust made visible. **Loop:** one more battle to discover their next combination. **Feeds:** Bonds, fear, skill chains.

- **2. The Promise Changes the Objective.** A hero dreaming of bringing someone home gains a contextual chain when that person falls: break pursuit → lift ally → cast while carrying. Each step uses learned skills, positioning and interruptible windows. **Do:** route the rescue and order teammates to make openings. **Screenshot:** your mage carries their rival through fire while casting backward. **Feel:** “They meant it.” **Loop:** attempt the deeper expedition that could fulfill the promise. **Feeds:** Dreams, Bonds, rescue.

- **3. The Missing Beat.** A survivor retains their dead friend’s half of a combo as an unanswered timing window. An apprentice who learned the missing technique can answer it with their own variation. **Do:** slot the inherited skill and time the handoff; the survivor responds with the follow-through they stopped using. **Screenshot:** an old formation closes around a new face. **Feel:** grief becoming connection without replacing the dead. **Loop:** train the apprentice for that first successful answer. **Feeds:** grief, traditions, graves, chains.

- **4. The Nemesis Remembers Your Hands.** A recurring enemy recognizes a chain that scarred them and visibly prepares its counter. A frightened veteran recognizes the enemy’s killing windup earlier, revealing the escape opportunity they once missed. **Do:** bait the remembered chain, branch into another learned skill, then pilot the veteran through the opening. **Screenshot:** the scarred hero stands inside the old death pattern and interrupts it. **Feel:** earned reversal. **Loop:** hunt the next encounter as both combatants learn. **Feeds:** Nemesis, fear, telegraphs, piloting.

- **5. Parliament Takes the Field.** The champion’s ultimate unfolds as a sequence of spectral contributors performing their actual signature skills. Each soul’s participation follows its known promise; forced souls can demand an action mid-fight before joining. **Do:** build the sequence, fulfill a demand—protect an apprentice, spare a surrendering foe—and trigger the newly available link. **Screenshot:** the champion becomes the center of a whole remembered squad. **Feel:** accumulated history becoming power. **Loop:** reconcile another soul to attempt another impossible chain. **Feeds:** sacrifice, Dreams, Parliament, Old World.

**C — You are the Door**

You are a conscious rift, anchored beneath the town. Summoning brings people through your body. Heroes can love you, accuse you, defend you—or seize your anchor and carry you elsewhere.

An overthrow makes your strongest hero your jailer and your next recruits fellow prisoners. **Do:** earn allies, direct an escape through squad combat, or accept a new covenant governing whom you may summon. **Feel:** dependence on people you once treated as possessions. **Loop:** win someone’s trust, then attempt liberation. Enemies hunt your anchor; a beloved town can evacuate *you*. **Feeds:** raids, opinions, Rifts, defiance, prestige.
