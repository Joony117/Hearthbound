- **A — The spine’s weakest point: heroes become most interesting when they die.** You have vengeance, hollows, graves, grief and history. Where is the moment I’m delighted someone is *alive*? Give heroes something they want to live long enough to do—and let me help them achieve it. Otherwise we’re building an excellent obituary generator.
- **The sharper contradiction is sacrifice.** The existing fantasy explicitly makes people both companions and raw material. Keep that ugliness; make the town recognize it. If I feed someone’s best friend into my champion and only the champion’s rank changes, “hero as a person” is cosmetic.
- **The missing emotional beat is reciprocity:** “I cared for this person; now they chose to care for me.” A hero voluntarily returning for a stranded squad can create more attachment than ten personality modifiers.

**C — Five new twists**

- **The SSS Is a Parliament of Ghosts.** Every sacrificed hero remains a distinct voice inside the recipient. **Do/feel:** assemble an absurdly powerful champion, then enter their inner world to bargain with the people you consumed—including friends who accuse you and volunteers who still believe in you. Program combos whose steps require those voices to cooperate. Pride becomes complicity. **Loop:** one more promotion to discover who your champion becomes; one more inner expedition to reconcile two souls. **Feeds:** #2, #3, #4, #7, #9. Death remains death; the survivor inherits its consequences.

- **Your Hero Founds the Enemy Town.** A beloved veteran can reject your rule, take willing companions and establish a rival settlement across the valley. **Do/feel:** visit, trade, raid, negotiate, or pilot their former friend into a confrontation. They train apprentices, shelter your deserters and might rescue your expedition despite hating you. Betrayal becomes wounded pride—and reluctant respect. **Loop:** check what *your* rival built; one more expedition toward reunion or war. **Feeds:** #1, #4, #5, #6, #9.

- **Summon the Person They Would Have Been.** A rift reveals an alternate living version of a hero: your timid baker as a tyrant, your legendary knight as an ordinary parent. **Do/feel:** summon them, house them beside their counterpart, and choose whose account of their life to believe. Neither replaces the other, including a dead original. Recognition becomes vertigo. **Loop:** one more summon to expose another possible life; check whether the two become friends or enemies. **Feeds:** #2, #4, #7, #8, #9.

Their former home becomes a heart; streets become skill paths. **Do/feel:** rebuild neighborhoods into a colossal combo chain, then pilot the entire town during a raid while residents keep its organs working.
